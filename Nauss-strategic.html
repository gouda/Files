From global engagement to strategic impact

NAUSS Strategic Technology Partnership Program

A structured program that brings the world's leading technology companies, universities, research centers and security institutions to NAUSS, and turns each engagement into a partnership with measurable outcomes.

The objective is notto organize visits
The objective isto convert engagement into partnerships and outcomes

Why now

The recent HUMAIN leadership visit demonstrated the value of focused, high-level engagement with global technology leaders.

NAUSS has an opportunity to build on this momentum and establish a structured program that brings together the world's leading technology companies, universities, research centers and security institutions.

Every engagement should leave NAUSS with a partnership, not a photo opportunity.

The program

Launch the NAUSS Strategic Technology Partnership Program, supported by a flagship engagement platform:

NAUSS Technology Leaders SeriesThe flagship platform for hosting selected global institutions and leaders

What NAUSS seeks

Each engagement should produce at least one tangible outcome:

  • Joint research programs
  • AI and cybersecurity projects
  • Technology pilots
  • Specialized training programs
  • Researcher and faculty exchange
  • Joint innovation labs
  • Research commercialization opportunities
  • Strategic access to global expertise

Engagement model

Each strategic engagement follows the same five-stage structure, from first meeting to a committed plan.

  1. Executive meetingLeadership alignment on shared priorities
  2. Research and technology dialogueExperts map areas for collaboration
  3. Student and researcher engagementDirect exchange with NAUSS talent
  4. Partnership workshopShape concrete joint initiatives
  5. 30-day action planOwners, timelines and next steps agreed

Every visit ends with defined owners, timelines and measurable next steps.
